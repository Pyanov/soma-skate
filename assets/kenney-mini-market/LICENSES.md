# Mini Market (Kenney): source and licence

Kenney's **Mini Market**, https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-market, downloaded 2026-10-18 (no account needed). Licence: **CC0 1.0** (public domain; no attribution required, credited here). The kit's own License.txt is copied below.

Used by the world generator (its city/kits.py, the OBJ and MTL files in its city/kits/kenney-mini-market/), baked into the sf export's mesh (assets/park/sf.city.*), drawn only (collision is the city's own boxes, grind lines explicit): interiors: the corner store's shelves, freezers, fruit and bread displays and the registers; fruit stands in front of bodegas.

Models: cash-register, shelf-bags, shelf-boxes, shelf-end, freezer, freezers-standing, display-fruit, display-bread, shopping-basket, bottle-return.
