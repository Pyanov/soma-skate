# Retro Urban Kit (Kenney): source and licence

Kenney's **Retro Urban Kit**, https://kenney.nl/assets/retro-urban-kit, downloaded 2026-10-18 (no account needed). Licence: **CC0 1.0** (public domain; no attribution required, credited here). The kit's own License.txt is copied below.

Used by the world generator (its city/kits.py, the OBJ and MTL files in its city/kits/kenney-retro-urban-kit/), baked into the sf export's mesh (assets/park/sf.city.*), drawn only (collision is the city's own boxes, grind lines explicit): construction sites and the DIY spot: scaffolding, pallets, planks, dumpsters, barriers.

Models: scaffolding-poles, scaffolding-structure, scaffolding-floor, pallet, pallet-small, planks, detail-dumpster-closed, detail-dumpster-open, detail-barrier-type-a.
