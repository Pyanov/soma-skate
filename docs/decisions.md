## 2026-10-18: Create-a-Skater
- **A created skater is a kit recipe on the full skater rig** (src/client/cas.ts, data/cas.json). The editor's choices (a piece id or a colour per row) become a kit recipe (kitpeople.ts) and a character spec. The rig comes from the build and height. The attitude sets the riding style: bend, arms, swing, squash, idles, celebrations, the catch flair and tweak. The special sets a pose-only signature with its own trick names on the HUD. The bail sets a pose-only bail style, and the taunt sets the emote. So a created skater rides with trick poses, bails, specials and squash like the hand-built characters, and the quality presets and reduce motion apply to it unchanged.
- **The signature prop is out only standing still, celebrating, in specials and taunts.** Riding with a cup in hand would fight the grabs and balance poses. While the prop is out, the skater view holds that hand in the prop's frame (grip or tray) so it stays upright.
- **The editor is an overlay in the picker's look** (src/client/caseditor.ts), opened from the picker's "+ Create-a-Skater" entry (N; L1 on the pad).
  - A single scrolling list of rows in sections, not tabs, so the pad needs only the menu keys it already sends: arrows, cross, circle, plus triangle to randomize.
  - Every row and arrow can be tapped.
  - The turntable stands, fidgets and now and then pops a kickflip. This is a pose-only loop on the view's Thing; the sim never sees it. Reduce motion slows the turn and drops the kickflip.
  - Up to 4 skaters are kept (soma-skate:cas), each as { id, name, choices, kit }. Leaving saves them, and they join the roster as cas1 to cas4.
- **Unlocks are data.** A piece's unlock takes a story mission (story), a sticker, Tour tapes or goals, and a hint. cas.ts awardPiece(id) gives one from anywhere (soma-skate:cas-awards). The Muni conductor's cap, Karl's sunglasses, the burrito costume and the Sea Lion's skipper cap, plus a few more, wait behind missions or stickers. A new piece is announced once. Locked pieces show their hint when you cycle onto them and are not worn.
- **The name shows on the HUD and on the results cards** for created skaters only, so the default HUD is unchanged. The postcard is the turntable shot from in front, framed on the photo mode's cream card ("Introducing", the name, the attitude and special) and downloaded.
- **Clips can ride a created skater:** `skate clip <run> --cas skaters.json --char cas1`. The created skaters are now loaded before the headless return, so shots and clips have them too.
- **The default and first launch are unchanged.** Create-a-Skater is optional and never blocks play.

## 2026-10-18: slow motion and trick cameras
- **Slow motion is a time scale in the frame loop, never in the sim.** Slow motion only means fewer of the sim's 120 Hz ticks per real second, so replays, hashes, scenarios and every clock in the sim stay the same. A test checks that a run gives the same world hash with and without the director. The director (src/client/director.ts, data/moments.json) steps once per tick from the skater and the tick's events, the same way the cameras do. So play, the instant replay, the results replay, the share clip and `skate clip` pick the same moments and shots. The attract mode's demos also get them, because they play through the clip API.
- **What counts as a big moment, and how slow.** A big moment is a flip or grab in an air, a special, a grind that will last (worked out from the rail left and the speed on it), or a gap. For an air, the director predicts the flight ballistically along its path and the ground under it, because off the Big 3 the ground drops away under the stairs. It eases to 0.4 speed in 0.12 s and holds about 0.7 s of real time centred on the peak. It eases back out in 0.2 s and is done before the landing. Big moments (the default) wait 6 s before the next one. Every trick has lower thresholds and a 2.2 s cooldown. On a two-minute Tour run that is 3 moments, or 5 with every trick.
- **The rule for time.** There is no slow motion and no trick camera in head-to-head or chase games: the hill bomb and Muni races, the Robotaxi Escape, S.K.A.T.E., and two-player or split screen. Timed solo runs (Tour levels, the 2-minute run) keep it. Their clocks count sim ticks, so the clock slows down with the slow motion: it neither costs nor gives time.
- **The shots.**
  - A flip or grab gets a low swing to the open side. The director tries both sides with the chase camera's line-of-sight test.
  - A big air (over 1.1 s and 2.3 m) gets a slow orbit through the peak.
  - A grind gets a push-in from ahead along the rail.
  - The camera swings in as the slow motion starts, so the swing itself plays slowed.
  - It holds through the landing, then goes back to the chase camera on a slower, critically damped spring.
  - The camera moves round the skater (blended in polar coordinates around them), not through them.
  - The chase camera's collision keeps the shot out of walls, cars and rails. That collision eases in with the swing, so leaving the chase camera never jumps.
  - The shots only ride over the chase camera. The Trick cameras setting and Reduce motion turn them off.
- **Sound.** The effects and ambience go through a low-pass (1500 Hz at full slow motion). New sounds and the rolling, grinding and wind loops play pitched to 0.62. Music and voices are untouched. Clips repeat frames through the slow motion, and their offline sound puts each event at its stretched time, pitched the same way.

## 2026-10-17: scoring integrity
- **The classic games repetition curve, with a floor, by trick id for every entry.** scoring.repeat [1, 0.75, 0.5, 0.25, 0.1] replaces halving per repeat. Halving made a repeat worth almost nothing, so a chain of one link grew only with its length; the floor makes it grow with its square, as in the classic games, which is why the farms below had to be closed rather than left to the curve. Gaps, spins and reverts repeat by id like tricks, so a re-hit gap degrades too.
- **A link cannot repeat itself for nothing.** One revert per landing, one lip trick per air, at most two wallrides per air and a wall never lifts you, a powerslide does not link to the slide just before it and a too-short slide banks instead of holding the combo open, a skitch under 1 s does not count and grabbing again straight after one banks first. Each was found by mashing the move: the farm paid tens to hundreds of thousands of points for one landing or one car.
- **A regrind comes back down.** While still rising after leaving a grind, only a line at least 0.1 m higher can be caught (a transfer up). Popping off a rail, or off one edge of a ledge onto the other, and catching it the next tick was seven grinds a second. Height rather than "the same line" because the ping-pong between a ledge's two edges was the same farm.
- **Balance gets touchier with each link of the same kind in the combo (later classic games).** Each grind (lip, manual) already in the combo starts the next one further over and tips it faster, so even perfect presses lose a chain of about 28. Short hops escaped the old time ramp entirely (a 14-tick grind never tips), so the start tilt is what caps them.
- **Manuals start from 2 m/s, not from walking pace.** A manual that stops at once banked its base points: tapping W, S standing still paid 942 a second. The minimum to keep one going stays 0.8 m/s.
- **Gaps that anyone can hop on the spot need travel or the right takeoff.** Air gaps take minMeters (WAVE AIR 3 m, the crest airs 8 m); Dolores's crest airs take off on the crest at 19th instead of anywhere in a 290 m box (every full ollie on Dolores paid 3,200). CAR HOP pays once per car in 30 s and NEAR MISS once per walker in 15 s, the same object being farmed rather than the move.
- **`skate farm`, a random search, alongside scripted mashing.** Scripted patterns only find what one thinks of; the random patterns at every spot found the slide-tap link and the ledge-edge ping-pong. Kept as a tool, not a check step: its output is read by hand (a long legit chain on a dense park looks like a farm to a threshold).
- **Tiers re-measured, Hayes moved a lot.** SoMa's Sick goes to 52,000 (the second manual is worth 75% now); Hayes Valley to 34,000 / 107,000 because its best line now carries on from the short seat wall onto the long one beside it. The method is unchanged (Pro about a decent run, Sick about 84% of the best line every 10 s); the other three levels did not move.

## 2026-10-17: race tick budget
- **Speed-ups that keep every number the same, proved rather than hoped.** A race tick (80 cars, 4 park NPCs, 3 racers) cost about 0.08 ms on average and its p99 had reached 0.46 ms on a busy machine; most of it was 40 small sphere queries walking the city's half-million-node BVH, and every car scanning every other car. All four changes below return bit-identical results, and all 155 scenarios, both races and the replays hash exactly as before.
- **Sphere queries answer from a few cached neighbourhoods** (16 per park, scratch, never world state). Each holds the BVH leaves that overlap a box 0.25 m bigger than the query that made it, in the walk's order, with copies of their triangles' corners and bounds. A walk reaches a leaf exactly when the leaf's box overlaps the query box, so a query inside a cached box reaches only cached leaves, in the same order; triangles left out of a neighbourhood are ones its box test would reject anyway. 97% of race queries hit. Rejected: a looser cache (any triangle near the box), which could differ from the walk in the last bit for a triangle exactly at the radius.
- **A triangle-box test before the closest-point maths** (padded 1e-6 m, far above the closest point's rounding), and **one interleaved array for BVH node boxes** (the left child is the next node), so a box test reads one cache line, not two.
- **Car following through a grid of the cars' tick-start positions** (cells follow.range + 5 m, an open-addressed table of typed arrays) plus the cars that jumped more than 4 m this tick (respawns, dead ends). Every car within range of the follower is in its 3 x 3 cells or on the jumped list, and Math.min does not care about order or repeats, so each car's target speed is the same number. A path position is found by binary search instead of a walk along the street's points (the first point past s, the same index).
- **The race perf case takes each tick's best of three runs.** The race is deterministic, so every run does the same work tick for tick; the best time per tick removes the other programs on the machine (other builds running at the same time made raw p99 swing between 0.2 and 1 ms for the same code). `check --full` fails if either race's p99 goes over 0.3 ms; `skate perf --race` also prints the last run's raw numbers.

## 2026-10-16: board physics
- **Grounded board physics, arcade tricks untouched.** Goal: the characters can look funny and eclectic, but the physics and the skateboard must be realistic and feel good. Rolling, turning, slopes and landings now follow docs/mechanics/board.md; ollie heights, spins, grinds, manuals and combos keep their arcade numbers.
- **Steering is a lean with finite rates, and the curve opens with speed:** kappa = u / (minRadius + v^2 / carveAccel). The keyboard is digital, so the lean (not the key) is the analogue state: a tap is a small carve, a hold a hard one. The speed term is the rider's (and the bushings') limit; it puts a full-lean carve at about 0.85 g at speed, the grip edge.
- **Grip is a friction budget on the sideways velocity only** (mu * N * dt per tick, static then kinetic). Inside it, the velocity is rotated with the board, so a turn keeps its speed (the old lerp shortened the velocity every tick: about 16% speed lost per second of hard turning at 6 m/s). Past it, the rest is slip that the sliding friction scrubs. The board may drift at most carveSlipMaxDeg off the travel direction while steering, so over-steering at speed is a scrubbing drift, not a spin-out.
- **Normal load from the turning surface normal** (gravity's share plus v times the normal's turn rate per tick). It feeds the grip, rolling resistance and the pump. Measured against the smoothed up after its update: the raw contact normal leads the smoothed one by about two ticks of rotation and read twice the true v^2 / R.
- **Pump = pressing against the extra load** (crouchPump * (load - n.z) * g along travel, both into and out of a transition). No curvature, no pump, so on a hill crouching is only a tuck. Calibrated as about 0.3 m of leg extension per arc.
- **Air drag everywhere** (0.5 rho CdA v^2 / m, CdA 0.45 standing sideways-on, 0.25 tucked, 80 kg rider and board) replaces the 1%/s air damping; rolling resistance is Crr * N with Crr 0.010, the old flat-ground deceleration. Drag is what makes a hill's terminal speed; it also costs a 9 m/s approach to the vert ramp about 3 J/kg, so the lab vert scripts were retimed (they reach the coping 6 to 13 ticks later and lower).
- **Coping rule: on a wall steeper than vert.minWallDeg, rising past a walkable top in reach launches.** Gravity pulls along a vertical wall, never into it, so a slow skater rolled over the coping onto the deck before. Measured on the 3.4 m lab vert: 7-8 m/s stall below the lip, 9 m/s gets 1.8 m/s of air, 10 m/s 4.3 m/s.
- **Landing quality by angle and impact speed** (clean / sketchy / stumble / bail) instead of angle alone: stumble from 7 m/s into the surface (a 2.5 m drop to flat), bail from 9.5 m/s (4.6 m). Landing on a transition is soft because the impact is only the speed into the surface.
- **Powerslide on Shift (gamepad L2; revert moves to R2 only).** Shift is free, held by the left hand next to WASD, and the conventional drift key. The foot brake (S) fades with speed, so stopping from speed is a slide, as on a real board.
- **Rider-line autopilot rides like a person:** pure pursuit along the line and a foot brake before corners tighter than its speed can turn. With grip limits, a 47 degree corner in 4.4 m at 6.7 m/s is not possible (it was at 1.6 g).
- **Flat tops of walls and fence rails are rideable; a skater wedged in the air for 0.5 s goes back to its last solid ground.** Fuzz (seed 5, 200k ticks) dropped the skater off a bank onto the top of W-NORTH, where it hung "in air" forever (wall faces were never rideable), then into a 24 cm slot between fence_NE and the invisible boundary wall. The old code soft-locks there too (soma-walltop-stuck). The slot itself is park geometry, a fix for the world generator's park model.
